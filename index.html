The mission

Buoys ping. Satellites pass. Models run. The ocean never stops talking — we make it legible. Chartr fuses open marine data into one living chart, so every skipper, scientist and coastal team can read the water at a glance.

04Who we are

An ocean-tech studio, not a dashboard factory.

Chartr is an independent ocean-tech studio. We build the interface the ocean always deserved — taking the open data our coasts already produce and engineering it into a product that feels inevitable.

No black boxes. No PDFs from 2004. Just open infrastructure, real-time signal and design that respects the people making decisions on the water.
